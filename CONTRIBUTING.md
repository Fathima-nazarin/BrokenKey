# Contributing to KeyRush

## Independent-work requirements

- All investigation and implementation must be your own.
- Do not use AI to inspect, diagnose, explain, solve, test, review, or modify any KeyRush bug or feature.
- Do not submit AI-generated code, AI-generated tests, AI-generated explanations, or work produced by another participant.
- Do not share challenge source, screenshots, issue descriptions, or solutions with AI tools.
- Use ordinary debugging tools and official documentation where permitted by the event rules.
- Ask an organizer before using a tool or resource if its status is unclear.

## Required submission evidence

For each change, include:

1. **Observed behavior:** What did you see?
2. **Reproduction:** The steps you personally followed to reproduce it.
3. **Root cause:** Explain the cause in your own words and point to the relevant file/function.
4. **Change summary:** Describe what you changed and why.
5. **Verification:** List the manual checks and edge cases you personally tested.
6. **Limitations:** Mention anything you did not verify.

Keep changes focused. Use incremental commits that reflect your actual work; do not fabricate commit history.

## Live verification

Organizers may ask you to:

- Reproduce the original behavior.
- Walk through your change and explain the relevant logic.
- Demonstrate the fix in a browser.
- Make a small related modification without assistance.
- Explain an edge case or a test result.

You must be able to explain every submitted line. If you cannot demonstrate independent understanding, the submission may be referred for organizer review.

## Declaration

By submitting, you confirm:

> I completed this work independently. I did not use AI assistance or another person's solution to inspect, diagnose, implement, test, or review any KeyRush bug or feature. I can explain and demonstrate the work I am submitting.
